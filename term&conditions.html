1. Who these terms apply to

These terms apply to anyone who uses this website (avoradigital.online) or submits a project inquiry through it, and to clients who go on to engage Avora Digital for a project. By submitting the contact form or confirming a quote, you agree to these terms for that engagement.

2. The services we offer

Avora Digital provides Shopify development, custom web development, Facebook and Instagram marketing, Meta Ads management, logo and label design, and — for clients in Pakistan — printing and delivery of custom-branded bottles and tissue paper. For clients outside Pakistan, packaging work is limited to design only; we do not currently offer international printing or shipping.

3. Pricing and quotes

Prices shown on our Pricing page are starting prices (marked with "+") for each tier, not fixed final prices. The exact price for your project depends on its scope and is confirmed in a written quote before any work begins. Every quote sets out the deliverables, number of revision rounds, and timeline for that project — those details apply once we both agree to them, not the general figures on the pricing page.

Submitting the project form on our Contact page is a free inquiry — it does not commit you to anything or require payment. Prices can be viewed in USD or PKR on the pricing page; PKR figures are rounded estimates, and your actual invoice is issued in whichever currency is agreed in your quote.

4. Payment

Payment terms (including any deposit, payment schedule, and accepted payment methods) are confirmed with you individually as part of your quote, since they can vary by project size and client location. Ongoing services billed monthly, such as social media management or Meta Ads, are invoiced on the schedule agreed when the engagement starts.

5. Revisions and changes to scope

The number of revision rounds included in your project is set out in your quote. Requests that go beyond the agreed scope or revision count — for example, a substantially different design direction after approval — may be treated as additional work and quoted separately before we proceed.

6. Delivery, printing and shipping

Digital deliverables (websites, Shopify stores, ad campaigns, logo and label design files) are delivered electronically. Physical production — printing and delivery of bottle labels and tissue packaging — is currently only available to clients based in Pakistan. Delivery timelines for physical goods depend on the print run and courier and are confirmed at the time of order.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Providing the content, brand assets, access or information we reasonably need to do the work.
  • Giving timely feedback during revision rounds, since delays on your end can push back the delivery timeline.
  • Making sure any content, logos or material you provide to us are yours to use, or that you have the rights to use them.
  • Paying invoices by the terms agreed in your quote.

8. Ownership of deliverables

Once a project is paid in full, ownership of the final, agreed deliverables (the completed website, final logo files, final ad creatives, etc.) transfers to you. Avora Digital may keep and reuse general know-how, techniques and any unused concepts or drafts from the project, and may showcase completed work in our own portfolio unless you ask us not to.
