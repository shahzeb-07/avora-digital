Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who we are

Avora Digital is a Pakistan-based digital and branding agency offering Shopify development, custom web development, Meta Ads and social media marketing, and logo, label and packaging design. This policy explains what information this website (avoradigital.online) collects and how it is used. If you have questions, contact us at hello@avoradigital.online.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. The main way this happens is through the project form on our Contact page, which can include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company or brand name (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your estimated budget range
  • A description of your project or requirements
  • A phone or WhatsApp number, if you choose to share one

We do not ask for payment details anywhere on this website, and we do not collect information beyond what you submit through the form.

How we use this information

We use the details you submit only to respond to your inquiry, understand your project, and prepare a quote or next steps. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to your request.

Third-party services we use

This website relies on a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • FormSubmit — the contact form is delivered to our inbox using FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-processing service. When you submit the form, your information passes through FormSubmit's servers on its way to our email. We don't control FormSubmit's own data practices; you can review their policy at formsubmit.co.
  • Google Analytics 4 — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). Your IP address is anonymized before it is processed. This only runs if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner — see the next section.

Cookies

We use a small amount of browser storage for two things:

  • Remembering your cookie choice (accepted or rejected), so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • If you accept, loading Google Analytics so we can see overall site usage. This is off by default and only turns on after you click "Accept Analytics" in the cookie banner.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie icon in the bottom corner of any page, or by clearing your browser's site data for avoradigital.online.

How long we keep your information

We keep the details from your project inquiry for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, to deliver the project. If you'd like us to delete the information you've submitted, email hello@avoradigital.online and we will remove it from our records.

Your choices

  • You can reject analytics cookies at any time.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • You can simply not submit the contact form if you'd rather not share your details — browsing the site doesn't require it.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for business inquiries and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at hello@avoradigital.online.
